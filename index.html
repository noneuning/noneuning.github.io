Questions people actually ask us

Does Google Maps work in Korea?

Partially. Korean law restricted export of high-precision map data for years, so walking and driving directions have been unreliable. The government approved conditional export in February 2026, but the improvement is not fully live yet. For now, most locals use Naver Map or KakaoMap. Google Maps is fine for finding a place; it's less reliable for getting there on foot.

What days are museums closed in Seoul?

Most museums and art galleries close on Mondays, including the Seoul Museum of Art. Palaces have their own closing days — Gyeongbokgung closes Tuesdays, Deoksugung closes Mondays. Last admission is usually one hour before closing, which catches a lot of people out.

How hot is Seoul in August?

Daytime highs sit around 33–36°C with very high humidity. The real problem in Gwanghwamun is that the main square has almost no shade. If you're visiting in August, plan indoor stops between roughly 1 PM and 4 PM rather than pushing through.

Can I book restaurants without a Korean phone number?

Yes, but not through every app. Naver and the domestic version of CatchTable require Korean phone verification. CatchTable Global lets you sign up with an email address. Many walk-in-only restaurants use a tablet system at the door that only works with a Korean app — for those, arriving early is the only real strategy.
